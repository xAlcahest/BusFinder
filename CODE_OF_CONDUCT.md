# Code of conduct

This is a small project run by volunteers, for people who ride buses. The
expectations are the ones you would have on the bus.

**Be decent.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. No harassment, no slurs,
no personal attacks, no unwelcome attention of any kind, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else the project shows up.

**Be useful.** Bug reports with steps beat rants. Reviews that say what is
wrong and why beat reviews that just say no. If something is not to your
taste, say so once and move on.

**Assume good faith, up to a point.** Most friction is a misunderstanding.
When it is not, the maintainer will step in: a warning first, then removal
from the conversation, then a block. Deliberate abuse skips the first two.

If you see behaviour that crosses the line, or you are on the receiving end
of it, report it privately through GitHub's report feature on the offending
content, or through the private vulnerability reporting channel in
`SECURITY.md` if you would rather not use the public tools. Reports are read
by the maintainer only.
